# Parts layout and expanded explosion

Open the local [Engine Lab](http://127.0.0.1:5173/) and refresh to load this round.

## Explore the collection

Choose **Arrange parts**, the **Parts** view tab, or press **G**. The engine separates, then moves into a grid organized by system. The complete collection contains **675 selectable component identities**: **587 bodies from the purchased assembly** and **88 authored teaching parts**.

Use the system list in the inspector to zoom into a group. Orbit and zoom normally; click a part for its details or double-click it to isolate it. **Fit all parts** returns to the collection. **Back to assembly** restores the physical presentation. The **Internals** toggle includes or hides the teaching parts.

Each component is uniformly resized to its own display cell. Its shape is preserved, but sizes and distances between parts in this view are **not physical measurements**. The layout uses a fixed inspection pose and pauses cycle motion. The purchased exterior and illustrative teaching mechanism remain distinct evidence layers.

## Separate the assembly

**Explode assembly** retains the mechanical arrangement and now offers **three times the former maximum displacement**. The separation slider remains available at **0%**, including while dragging or using Home/End and arrow keys, so you can move freely between assembled and exploded states.

The grid is a separate presentation: choose it when you want every component to have its own inspection space. Use Assembly to compare proportions and the working cutaway to follow the mechanism.
