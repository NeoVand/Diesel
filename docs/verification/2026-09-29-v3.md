## Lighting and material evidence

The purchased OBJ has neither UV coordinates nor tangent vectors. Anisotropic source-metal shading depended on that missing surface basis and produced white patches. Source finishes now use isotropic reflections, with a controlled studio environment, directional key/fill/rim lights, roughness and contact shadows.

A same-camera top-view comparison at 2336 × 1760 output pixels counted **14,269 near-white pixels before and zero after** disabling unsupported anisotropy (all RGB channels at least 245). This specific comparison supports the observed artifact fix; it is not an assertion that every future camera or material can never produce a bright highlight. The before/after images and measurement record are retained.

The renderer retains **587 purchased source groups**, **965,744 runtime source triangles**, and **88 authored teaching component records**. The renderer check records:

- All twelve teaching cylinder banks and 259 teaching pickable meshes visible during exploded assembly; none visible when combined-view internals are disabled.
- Source raycast surfaces match section rendering on exposed backfaces. An actual canvas double-click returned the expected frontmost source identity.
- Section cap eligibility restored after explosion/reassembly: 14 source records before and after.
- Every projected corner of an isolated, removed piston stays inside the fitted camera view.
- The separate cylinder study presents one cylinder. Whole-engine section remains a distinct display.
- Zero recorded renderer/browser errors during this check.

Closed intersection contours can generate cut faces with nested holes. Open contours remain unfilled. With teaching internals enabled, the unverified main casting shows a cut boundary instead of a generated slab that would hide the mechanism. This policy does not recover authentic bores, passages or wall thicknesses.

## Rendering measurements

Installed headless Chrome used WebGL 2 / ANGLE Metal on **Apple M4**, with a 1168 × 880 CSS-pixel canvas. Requested pixel ratio was 2, capped by the renderer at **1.25**. AO uses half-resolution targets and eight samples; shadows use a 2048² map refreshed once per frame.

A warmed, uncontended 119-frame-interval sample with motion and **all five flow systems enabled** measured:

| View                       | Median interval | 95th percentile | Mean draw calls across passes | Mean triangles across passes |
| -------------------------- | --------------: | --------------: | ----------------------------: | ---------------------------: |
| Whole-engine section       |         16.7 ms |         16.8 ms |                           930 |                    3,691,355 |
| Exposed teaching mechanism |         16.7 ms |         16.7 ms |                           663 |                      636,549 |

Both samples were approximately 60 FPS on the named machine. These short samples are not a universal device guarantee or long-duration GPU-memory test. The earlier integrated verification sample ran while type/lint checking was active and measured section median 33.3 ms / 95th percentile 50 ms; that record is retained rather than overwritten. CPU rebuilding of 21 plane positions in that integrated run measured median 22.0 ms and 95th percentile 29.2 ms; this is separate from steady animation frame timing.

## Live guide

The actual Codex SDK/native harness completed a paid live request through browser-scoped MCP tools. The request asked for a whole-engine Z section at **40%**, flipped false, guide hidden, teaching internals enabled and motion paused. Two browser commands received **applied** acknowledgements; the API returned HTTP 200 with acknowledged execution. The final UI independently matched every requested setting, and the guide explicitly distinguished the purchased exterior from the approximately placed educational internals. No page errors were recorded.

The sanitized acknowledgement/state record and final guide screenshot are saved with this report. They contain no API key or session bearer token.

## Fidelity boundaries

The moving internals, connecting-rod length, representative phasing, detailed valve/injection timing, finishes and fluid routes are educational. Embedded placement and scale are inferred from repeated source bank-cover geometry and are not an OEM internal registration. The published nominal dimensions and fixed-1800-rpm EM1898-00 performance map remain separately sourced; no calibrated combustion, CFD, transient, structural or engine-acoustic prediction is claimed.

Earlier live GPT Audio 1.5 narration remains documented in the historical v2 record. Audio implementation was not changed in this rendering update; browser playback/stop fixtures were rerun, but another paid audio synthesis was not needed.
